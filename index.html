About Gunita Pamana

A Legacy Made For Memories

Gunita Pamana Hotel is a 4-star hotel located in Makati, Manila, offering comfortable accommodations, elegant facilities, quality service, and genuine Filipino hospitality.

Our hotel brings together the warmth of Filipino culture and the comfort expected from a modern hotel. Every detail is thoughtfully designed to make guests feel welcomed, valued, and cared for.

The name Gunita Pamana reflects two meaningful ideas: memory and legacy. It represents our desire to create experiences that guests can carry with them long after their stay.

“Where every stay becomes a cherished memory.”

Our History

A Legacy of Hospitality

The Gunita Pamana Hotel embodies the evolution of accommodation establishments in the Philippines by blending international standards with Filipino hospitality.

Makati
Manila

Rooted in the development of Manila's tourism industry, Gunita Pamana represents the growth of modern accommodation establishments that serve both business and leisure travelers.


Over time, the hotel has adapted to modern expectations by incorporating sustainable practices, digital check-in systems, and guest-centered innovations while maintaining the warmth of Filipino service.


Positioned in Makati, one of the country's major business districts, Gunita Pamana serves as a bridge between tradition and modernity. The hotel offers travelers a reliable standard of comfort while showcasing the cultural heritage suggested by its name, “Pamana,” meaning legacy.
